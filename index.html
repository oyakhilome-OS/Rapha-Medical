Frequently asked questions

Do you accept medical aid?+

Yes — we accept Discovery, Bonitas, Momentum, Medshield and most major South African medical aid schemes. Bring your membership card to your first visit and our front desk will confirm your benefits before you're seen.

How quickly can I get an appointment?+

Routine appointments are typically available within 24–48 hours. We also hold a small number of same-day urgent slots each morning for anything that can't wait.

Can I register as a new patient?+

Absolutely. New patients are welcome — book directly via WhatsApp or email, and we'll set up your file ahead of your first visit.

Do you see children and infants?+

Yes, our GPs see patients of all ages, from newborns through to seniors, including routine vaccination schedules.

What should I bring to my first appointment?+

Your ID, medical aid card, and a list of any current medications. If you're transferring from another practice, prior records are helpful but not required.
